Cozy Harvest - Aseprite file

Cozy Harvest.aseprite holds every piece of the kit at its real pixel size, ready to polish by hand:
- Each piece is a slice named after it (panel, button_hover, cursor_normal...). Pick the Slice tool
  (Shift+C) to see them; double-click a slice to edit its 9-slice centre and pivot.
- Resizable pieces already have their 9-slice centre set; cursors have their click point as the pivot.
- The palette holds the kit's colours.
- Paint on the "Kit" layer, or on "Details" above it to keep your changes separate.

Bring it back into Bevelsmith: in the "Your Aseprite art" panel, click "Open Aseprite file".
Every piece you changed replaces the generated one everywhere - the preview, Test it and every engine
export - and anything you didn't draw keeps coming from the style. Keep the slice names; you can move
and resize slices freely, and change their 9-slice centres.

Drawing a kit from scratch works the same way: make a slice for each piece you draw, name it like the
pieces below, give resizable pieces a 9-slice centre, and open the file in Bevelsmith.
